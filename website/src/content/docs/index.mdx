CTXone is a self-hosted **context engine**: one Hub your whole toolchain
points at, giving every AI tool a shared, durable brain. It spans six
primitives — **memory**, **plans**, **branches**, **taint**,
**provenance**, and **token accounting** — over four surfaces (Hub,
Engine, Lens, and the `ctx` CLI). See the full tour in
[Features & commands](/reference/features/).
